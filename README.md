## About Me

I'm **Thomas**, a developer from **Greece** and the **CEO & Founder of No Limit Scripts**.

My main focus is **FiveM Modding & Development**, but I'm interested in software development as a whole. I enjoy building systems that combine programming, game development, UI/UX, databases, and different technologies into complete products.

I enjoy taking projects from the ground up. starting with an idea, planning the architecture, developing the systems, designing the interface, integrating everything together, and turning it into something people can actually use.

I'm always looking for new technologies, better development practices, and new ways to push the quality of the projects I work on.

## Engineering

I enjoy working across different parts of a project rather than limiting myself to one area.

### Backend

Server-side logic, data handling, APIs, resource communication, events, exports, callbacks, authentication, and database integration.

### Frontend

Modern interfaces, NUI development, interactive menus, HUDs, profiles, shops, statistics, animations, and other game interfaces.

### Game Systems

Gameplay mechanics, multiplayer systems, matchmaking, progression, competitive systems, player management, and custom game modes.

### Databases

Designing and integrating SQL-based systems for storing player data, statistics, progression, configuration, and other persistent information.

### Integration

Connecting different resources and systems together through APIs, exports, callbacks, events, databases, and shared interfaces.

## How I Work

### 01 Idea

Everything starts with a concept.

I figure out what the product should accomplish and what problem it should solve.

### 02 Planning

I plan the architecture, systems, integrations, database structure, and overall user experience.

### 03 Design

The interface and user experience are designed around the functionality of the system.

### 04 Development

The actual systems, logic, UI, integrations, and database functionality are built.

### 05 Testing

Features are tested, connected together, optimized, and refined.

### 06 Polish

Performance, UX, visuals, edge cases, and small details are improved.

### 07 Release

The finished product is packaged and prepared for real users.
